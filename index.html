Privacy Policy / Политика конфиденциальности

Updated / Обновлено: 2026-09-28

PlanSolo (app PlanS, operated by Lab.PhotoHub) is a private dashboard used by the owner of the Instagram professional account @nadina_solo to send automatic replies in Instagram Direct when a person writes a code word in a comment, a direct message or a story reply. The service is not offered to other businesses.

What data we process

  • Instagram-scoped ID and username of the person who commented or wrote a message;
  • the text of that comment or message and its time;
  • the ID of the post the comment belongs to;
  • for the connected account only: its ID, username and an access token (stored encrypted on our server).

Why

Only to match the code word and send the requested reply (for example, a link to a guide), to respect Instagram limits (24-hour messaging window, one private reply per comment) and to show the account owner a log of sent replies. We do not sell, share or use the data for advertising or profiling.

Retention

Event records are deleted automatically after 90 days. The access token is deleted when the account is disconnected.

Your rights and deletion

You can request deletion of your data at any time: remove the app in Instagram settings (Meta sends us a deletion request automatically) or write to @nadina_solo in Instagram Direct. See Data deletion.
